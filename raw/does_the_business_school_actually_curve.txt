u/WallStreetBound: Yes, the business school strictly enforces a target GPA curve across all core undergraduate classes—typically capping the average section GPA at a 3.0. How individual professors handle it varies completely. 
u/AccountingKing: Exactly. While some macroeconomics professors will apply a generous upward curve to save the class if the midterm average was a 55%, accounting and finance professors are notorious for doing the opposite. They will strictly cap the total number of 'A' grades allowed per section to stay compliant with the curve, meaning even if you earn a 92% raw score, you could get bumped down to a B+ if too many students scored higher than you.
