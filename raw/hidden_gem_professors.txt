Blog Post: The Hidden Gems of Campus Faculty
When looking for electives, students often flock to giant lecture halls, but the real secret to maintaining your GPA is hunting down passionate, highly-rated professors teaching niche or under-enrolled subjects.

Professor Evelyn Thorne in the Anthropology department is a prime example. She teaches "History of Myth and Magic," an under-enrolled course that students rave about. Her teaching style is entirely conversational, and she stands out because she evaluates students through creative multimedia projects instead of traditional exams. Similarly, Dr. Alan Cross in Linguistics makes complex syntax rules incredibly accessible and sets an incredibly low-stress pacing where homework is rarely assigned.
