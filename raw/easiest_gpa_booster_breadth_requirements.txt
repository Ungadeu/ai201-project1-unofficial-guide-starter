You need to take Introduction to Folklore with Professor Davis or Global Cinema with Professor Martinez. Both of these professors are highly recommended for electives because they focus entirely on class engagement and discussion over rote memorization. They stand out as low-stress because the grading criteria is entirely based on short, 200-word weekly discussion posts on the online portal rather than high-stakes midterms or final exams. I passed Martinez's class with a solid A just by showing up, watching the indie films, and participating in the group chats. Truly zero stress.
