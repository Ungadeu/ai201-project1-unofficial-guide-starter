Introductory writing seminars are a complete roll of the dice depending on which graduate Teaching Assistant (TA) you pull. While the official curriculum is identical, the actual grading rigor and weekly reading assignments vary wildly. 

TA Sarah's section requires three drafts per essay with intensive line-by-line feedback, whereas TA Mark's section is mostly graded on completion but has mandatory lecture attendance. If you miss more than two writing workshops in Mark's section, your participation grade drops by a full letter. Treat the syllabus as a baseline, but use the first week to gauge your TA's individual grading quirks.
